# Confirmed official fuel-price notices

The browser and scheduled job share `src/lib/prices/publicSource.ts`. They read Ministry of Energy pages on `gov.il`, trying a direct request and then the public text reader. This is page ingestion, not a guaranteed official API or a forecast. The index discovers monthly and dated within-month notices; current, preceding and next-month URLs are also tried as fallbacks. A URL date is only a discovery hint.

A notice needs the explicit mainland 95 self-service maximum and an unconditional, dated midnight clause in the body. Supported clauses contain one calendar date or the consecutive dates on either side of midnight. The latter activates on the second date. Publication date, when printed, remains a separate calendar date; retrieval time is stored separately. Conditional excise scenarios, ambiguous dates, challenge pages and unavailable pages do not establish a future price.

`byFuelType.95.self.notices` stores the effective timestamp, price, source URL, publication date, retrieval time and retrieval route. Confirmed future notices remain outside legacy `current`, `history` and `scheduledHistory` until effective. The dated lookup reads these notices using Israel time, so an already received notice activates on the existing Home clock without waiting for a server write. Historical notices remain available even if the user did not open the app during a short-lived price period.

A mid-month delta requires a preceding dated event in the same month. A real month boundary can use the preceding month's authoritative legacy bucket. Undated monthly data cannot invent a missing early-month event. A duplicate same-price notice does not create another change. A quantity example uses only a trusted vehicle capacity, explicitly describes purchasing that number of litres at the regulated maximum, and rounds only the displayed total to whole shekels.

Future alerts require a source read within 48 hours. A notice first read more than 48 hours before its effective time also requires a recheck near activation. Successfully rereading a page that no longer confirms a notice last seen before activation writes a `withdrawn` tombstone with the newer check time. The tombstone survives Firestore recursive merges and normalization; an older browser cache cannot resurrect it. Network failure alone never withdraws a confirmed notice or changes the active price. This also covers a withdrawal learned after midnight; a price already verified by a read after activation remains historical evidence. Fresh confirmed evidence can restore a withdrawn notice.

The scheduled check runs every two hours; the browser checks on entry/resume and periodically while visible, with a two-hour success cache. GitHub schedules and public pages can be delayed. Advance alerts depend on the Ministry publishing a readable notice before the effective time. They do not promise advance warning for every change.

The focused unit tests cover the 7.77 → 7.35 → 7.85 transition, exact activation, DST, missing history, legacy data, manual overrides, source correction, withdrawal with recursive merge, stale-cache replay, and quantity copy. `e2e/official-price-notices.spec.ts` exercises the mounted Home card and price sheet across activation in the local emulator environment.
