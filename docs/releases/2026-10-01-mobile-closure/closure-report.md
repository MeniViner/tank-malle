`computeStats` totals every valid continuity-safe closed segment and separately flags anomalies; anomaly flags do not remove segments from measured totals. Forecast `buildConsumptionSamples` uses the same continuity-safe engine, positive-distance/litres samples, endpoint quality from provenance, and recency weights. Its ratio estimate winsorises rates to a band around the weighted median before forecast weighting; it does not delete unusual raw records. Measured aggregate and forecast are therefore intentionally different calculations. Neither confirms the affected owner's data accuracy without inspection.
