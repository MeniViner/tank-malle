# Google Play marketing artwork

The approved six-panel RTL panorama is kept in `panorama-template.html`. Its
three phone images are the same synthetic demo screenshots already published
in the original store artwork. Keep the panels, copy, crossing phones and order.

`node scripts/compose-store-branding.mjs /absolute/output/folder` prepares one
editable panorama, six 1080×1920 page viewports and a contact-sheet preview.
Capture each panel through the browser, saving JPEGs as
`store-assets/01-tank-malle-brand.jpg` through `06-tank-malle-brand.jpg`.

The app name is **טנק מלא**. **ניהול הוצאות הרכב** is the marketing tagline,
loaded with the approved car-wallet icon from the shared app identity. The
tagline is not part of the installed launcher label.
